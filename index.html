About Ascenda

A trading partner built for the way fashion works now.

Big factories want big orders. Most emerging and mid-size brands don't work that way anymore. Ascenda was built to close that gap — combining a curated network of knit and woven specialists in China with hands-on trade expertise.

Sourcing, not just selling

We match every style to the right mill and workshop — knit specialists for jerseys and rib structures, woven specialists for blouses, skirts and tailoring.

One team, end to end

From tech-pack review and fabric development to inline QC and export documents — one English-speaking team owns your order from start to shipment.

Made for EU & US brands

Familiar with REACH, CPSIA and Prop 65 testing workflows, size grading for Western markets, and DDP shipping to New York, Los Angeles, London and beyond.

What We Make

Knit · Jersey

Knit Dresses

Jersey, rib and interlock dresses — bodycons, midis and smocked styles with a soft hand-feel and stable shrinkage.

Knit · Sweater

Sweaters & Cardigans

Cotton, acrylic-blend and wool-mix knits: cable knits, cropped cardigans and fully-fashioned production on flat-bed and computerized machines.

Tops & Tees

How We Work

From tech pack to doorstep, in five steps.

Inquiry & Quote

Send tech packs or references. Quotation with fabric, trims and freight options within 48 hours.

Sampling

Protos and fit samples with photo updates. Revisions until the sample is right.

Bulk Production

Fabric inspection, inline QC at 20% and 60% progress, weekly status reports.

Final QC

AQL 2.5 final random inspection with report and photos before packing.

Shipping

Air, sea or express. Full export docs, DDP available to major EU & US destinations.
